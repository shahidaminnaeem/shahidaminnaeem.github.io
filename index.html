About

Shahid is a researcher in high-energy nuclear and particle physics, specializing in Scientific Machine Learning, Statistical Modeling, and Scientific Computing. His research at the Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider (RHIC) at Brookhaven National Laboratory (BNL) centers on understanding the universe’s most fundamental mysteries. Shahid combines expertise in numerical simulations and data-driven methodologies to develop innovative solutions for complex scientific challenges. With a background in high-energy physics and research development, he is committed to advancing scientific knowledge and creating a meaningful impact within the scientific community.

Let’s Connect

I’m passionate about collaboration, scientific outreach, and interdisciplinary research. Over the years, I’ve mentored students, contributed to research projects, and engaged with scientific communities to promote open knowledge.

Whether it’s co-authoring a paper, developing computational tools, or exploring new research directions, I’m always open to meaningful collaboration.

Research interests

QCD Spin Physics & Nucleon Structure

Transverse single-spin asymmetries (TSSA), transverse momentum dependent (TMD) distributions, proton spin structure, Sivers/Collins effects.

Jet Physics

Inclusive jet and dijet production, jet substructure, jet reconstruction algorithms, acoplanarity studies.

Electron-Ion Collider (EIC) Physics

Deep inelastic scattering, gluon/sea-quark imaging, ePIC detector performance and early-science studies.

Flavor Physics & Standard Model Phenomenology

Heavy-quark expansion, bulk observables, collision-geometry and nuclear-deformation effects, AMPT and other heavy-ion transport models, pure/semi-leptonic decay observables and tests of the Standard Model.

Physics Beyond the Standard Model

Exotic matter, magnetic monopole searches, early-universe phase transitions and electroweak symmetry breaking.

Computational & Statistical Methods for HEP

Large-scale experimental data analysis, systematic-uncertainty estimation, Bayesian and iterative unfolding, detector simulation (GEANT4), calibration and reconstruction in collider data.

Scientific Machine Learning

Data-driven solutions for complex scientific problems, particle identification, event reconstruction, background subtraction, predictive analytics, and statistical analysis.
